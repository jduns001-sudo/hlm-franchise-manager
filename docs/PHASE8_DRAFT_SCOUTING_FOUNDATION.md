# Phase 8 Draft & Scouting Engine Foundation

Mission 285 opens Phase 8 using Section 11 of the Master Specification.

The foundation preserves the central design rule that the engine may know actual prospect ability while organizations operate from imperfect scouting estimates. Multiple scouts may disagree.

Scout attributes: current ability accuracy, potential accuracy, region, league, position specialization, knowledge.

Scout personalities: traditional, analytics, skill-focused, defensive.

Reports are designed around ranges, confidence, strengths, weaknesses and projection.

The capability boundary includes assignments, resources, exposure, international prospects, multiple rankings, draft-board tags, interviews, combine, medical evaluation, lottery, persistent pick ownership, protections, draft-day trades, CPU drafting, risers/fallers/sleepers/busts, future prospect generation and prospect stories.

CPU drafting factors are needs, philosophy, scouting, personality, position, potential, development and risk. Highest Overall alone is explicitly not the drafting rule.

This mission does not invent scouting-error formulas, confidence scales, lottery odds, draft-order rules, CPU selection weights, prospect-generation distributions, or live persistence.
