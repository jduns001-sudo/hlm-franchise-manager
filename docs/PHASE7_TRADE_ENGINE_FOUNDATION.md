# Phase 7 Mission 272 — Transactions & Trade Engine Foundation

Mission 272 establishes the read-only vocabulary and system boundary for Phase 7. It follows Section 10 of the Master Specification: trades are contextual negotiations between organizations with different goals, needs, personalities and memories. Overall alone is never trade value.

This mission does not invent a valuation formula. It does not generate offers, negotiate, execute trades, move assets, retain salary, evaluate pick conditions, alter GameState, or persist anything. Those behaviors are layered onto this foundation in later controlled missions.
