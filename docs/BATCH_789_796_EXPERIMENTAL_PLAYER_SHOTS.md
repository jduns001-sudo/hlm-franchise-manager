# Accelerated batch 789–796: experimental player shots and box scores

Eight integrated research-only milestones:

1. **789:** Resolve active, contracted, healthy skaters by team.
2. **790:** Resolve active starting goalie candidate.
3. **791:** Weight skater shot assignments by overall rating.
4. **792:** Assign bounded hypothetical shots and goals.
5. **793:** Attribute simplified first and second assists.
6. **794:** Calculate goalie shots against, goals against and saves.
7. **795:** Assemble deterministic game-level player box score.
8. **796:** Audit shot, goal and goalie-save totals, readiness and state immutability.

This is a **research-only attribution layer** over already sampled hypothetical team scores. Goals are assigned to early shot events, shots use a simplified fixed range, and assists are simplified. It does **not** simulate actual possessions, shot quality, line shifts, penalties, player fatigue, goalie substitution, injuries, or full NHL statistics. No claim of calibrated realism is made.

No official games, saved player statistics, standings, Front Office changes, roster imports, or Phase 11 PR #764 changes. Dedicated CI and Foundation Tests must both pass before merge.
